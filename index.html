Annual Leave (30 Working Days)

⚖️

ISSE Annual Leave Entitlement Rules

• GL 07 & Above: 30 Working Days (Full)  |  15 + 15 (Split)

• GL 04 – 06: 21 Working Days (Full)  |  11 + 10 (Split)

• GL 03 & Below: 14 Working Days (Full)  |  7 + 7 (Split)

• Two-Week Advance Notice: Applications must be submitted at least 14 days prior to commencement.

• Annual Quota: Annual leave can strictly be granted once per calendar year, in one Full period OR split into two halves.

• Splitting: When split, the first half is taken before the second half. Both halves must fall in the same calendar year.

⚖️

ISSE Maternity Leave Rules

• Duration: 112 Working Days (Full Pay).

• Minimum Start Point: May commence 28 working days before expected delivery.

• Adoption: 84 Working Days for adoption of a child under 4 months.

• Requirement: A medical certificate showing the expected date of confinement is required.

• Note: Annual leave for that year is regarded as part of maternity leave under the stated rule.
